## Brand & Style

The visual identity marries warm civic goodwill with an elevated, literary botanical aesthetic. Designed for a circular economy social enterprise where reuse, donation, and community mutual aid intersect, the style moves away from generic charity aesthetics into an editorial, curated presence.

The tone evokes a thoughtfully run social goods atelier—grounded, trustworthy, generous, and deliberate. It balances:
- **Literary editorial authority**: Scholarly serifs convey institutional durability, heritage, and integrity.
- **Organic stewardship**: Warm, deep botanical greens anchor the interface in nature and renewal, contrasted against vibrant warm apricot and sun-baked amber highlights.
- **Architectural clarity**: Modern geometric body typography and measured grid structures ensure frictionless donation drop-offs, volunteering applications, and community commerce.

Visual motifs prioritize tactile paper-like warmth, delicate hairline boundaries, rich foliage hues, and subtle, humanistic proportions.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in nature's quiet resilience, illuminated by warm community radiance.

- **Primary (`#2D5A27`)**: A deep botanical laurel green. Serves as the core signature for navigation banners, primary action buttons, structural headings, and trust badges.
- **Secondary (`#D96B27`)**: An earthy persimmon orange. Used purposefully for impact metrics, primary donation CTAs, and community volunteer callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#F4A261`)**: Warm honey-apricot. Applied to micro-interactions, secondary tags, and highlighted campaign dates.
- **Neutral (`#2B2E2A`)**: Deep forest charcoal. Replaces harsh true blacks for body text and foundational layout elements to maintain soft, readable contrast against warm cream backgrounds.

### Surface System
- Base page canvas utilizes `earth-linen` (`#FBF9F4`) rather than harsh digital white.
- Containers and raised cards sit on `paper-tint` (`#F3EFE6`) or pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle warm sandstone borders (`#E5DEC9`) to evoke recycled stationery.
- Critical social sharing and donor appreciation elements adopt `sharing-badge` (`#FFF4EC`) with persimmon text accents.

## Typography

The pairing of **Newsreader** and **Manrope** establishes an authentic, thoughtful rhythm.

- **Newsreader** delivers an editorial sensibility suited for organizational mission statements, storytelling showcases, circular impact narratives, and campaign headings. When paired with high numerical stats, italic or regular weights provide a humanistic character.
- **Manrope** handles all transactional, navigational, and administrative interfaces. Its clean geometric sans structure guarantees legibility across donation item checklists, dispatch times, inventory prices, and account interfaces.
- **Microcopy & Metrics**: For numerical counters (e.g., kilograms of carbon saved, total items re-homed), use Newsreader with generous line heights to preserve editorial grace.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces calm, spacious structure reminiscent of well-designed civic broadsheets and editorial publications.

- **Grid Structure**:
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid with a maximum content container of `1240px`, flanked by `margin` (`3rem`) and `gutter` (`1.5rem`).
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `2rem` outer margin and `1.25rem` gutter.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid with `margin-mobile` (`1.25rem`) and `gutter-mobile` (`1rem`).

- **Vertical Cadence**:
  - Section blocks utilize generous vertical intervals of `space-xxl` (`4rem`) to allow stories of ecological impact to breathe.
  - Interactive elements and card interiors observe compact intervals (`space-sm` to `space-md`), keeping transactional flows dense and intelligible.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm textural layering and soft ambient shading rather than cold digital drop shadows:

- **Flat Tonal Hierarchy**: Depth is communicated primarily by stacking background tints (`earth-linen` base canvas -> pure white cards -> `paper-tint` nested sub-sections).
- **Hairline Framing**: Surfaces are bounded by `1px` subtle borders using `warm-sandstone` (`#E5DEC9`) at 80% opacity, providing structure without heavy contrast.
- **Ambient Botanical Shadows**: When floating dialogs, tooltips, or elevated cards appear, shadows are diffused and tinted with raw foliage pigments:
  - *Resting card*: `0 2px 8px rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.04)`
  - *Interactive hover*: `0 8px 24px rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.08), 0 2px 4px rgba(27, 46, 23, 0.03)`
  - *Dropdown & modal*: `0 16px 36px rgba(43, 46, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.04)`

## Shapes

The interface embraces a gentle, disciplined Soft (`roundedness: 1`) curvature.

- Standard buttons, text fields, and small badges use `0.25rem` (4px).
- Content cards, modular tiles, and modal windows scale up to `0.5rem` (8px).
- Badges and micro-status tags may feature full pills (`9999px`) only when indicating statuses like "Eco-Certified" or "Pickup Scheduled."
- This controlled, minimal curvature gives the system an artisanal, crafted finish—resembling cut linen cardstock rather than synthetic plastic bubbles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Botanical)**: Filled `#2D5A27` background, pure white text, `font-weight: 600`, border-radius `0.25rem`. Hover shifts to `#1A3617`. Focus ring is `2px` offset with `#487341`.
- **Secondary Action (Civic Warmth)**: Filled `#D96B27` background, pure white text. Reserved for primary conversion goals (e.g., "Schedule Box Pickup", "Give Monthly").
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, `1px` border in `#2D5A27`, text in `#2D5A27`. Hover fills with `rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.06)`.

### Cards & Story Containers
- **Product & Story Cards**: White surface background, `1px` solid `#E5DEC9` boundary, `0.5rem` radius. Image ratio is 4:3 with warm low-saturation image treatments. Card footer houses impact metrics (e.g., "Saved 2.4kg CO₂").
- **Hover Behavior**: Subtle lift with `translateY(-2px)` and the Ambient Botanical Shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Eco / Donation Badges**: High-contrast, friendly chips. Background `#FFF4EC` with `#D96B27` typography for urgent campaigns; `#EBF3EA` with `#2D5A27` typography for sustainability tags.
- **Padding**: `4px 10px`, typography set to `label-md`.

### Form Elements (Inputs, Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp `1px` border in `#E5DEC9`, white fill, `0.25rem` radius. On focus, boundary transforms to `#2D5A27` with a subtle outline glow. Placeholder text in `#737770`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: `18px` geometric boundaries. Checked state fills with `#2D5A27` displaying a clean white icon checkmark.

### Impact Metric Banners
- Dedicated editorial containers with deep botanical `#1A3617` backgrounds, `#FBF9F4` Newsreader typography, and tertiary `#F4A261` numerical accents. Used across homepages and campaign pages to demonstrate collective community action.
